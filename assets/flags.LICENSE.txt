flags.png
=========

250 country flags, rasterised into one 16-wide grid of 60x45 cells in
alphabetical ISO-3166 alpha-2 order. Both clients derive a flag's position
from that order, so the sprite and the code lists in index.html and
apps/mobile/src/lib/leaderboard.ts must be regenerated together.

Rebuilt from flag-icons 7.5.0 (4x3 set), quantised to 256 colours.

Source artwork: flag-icons by Panayiotis Lipiridis
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons
